Illinois (Statewide) January 1, 2024 Paid Leave for All Workers Act (PLAWA) (820 ILCS 192) All private employers in Illinois (unless covered by pre-existing qualified local ordinance) Nearly all employees, including domestic, part-time, temporary, and seasonal workers 1 hour per 40 hours worked, capped at 40 hours minimum per 12-month benefit period Accrues Day 1; eligible to use starting on the 90th day of employment Up to 40 hours carryover required unless employer frontloads 40 hours at year start Usable for any reason; employer cannot require documentation, verification, or reason No payout required under PLAWA unless governed by general vacation terms under Wage Payment Act
Nevada (Statewide) January 1, 2020 Nevada Revised Statutes (NRS § 608.0197) Private employers with 50 or more employees (exempts first 2 years of employer operation) All employees (full-time, part-time, temporary) working in Nevada 0.01923 hours per hour worked (approx 40 hours annually for 40 hr/week employee) Accrues Day 1; eligible to use on the 90th calendar day of employment Up to 40 hours carryover permitted per benefit year unless frontloaded by employer Any reason without requiring explanation or justification to employer No statutory payout requirement upon separation unless employer contract promises it
California (Statewide) Updated January 1, 2024 Healthy Workplaces, Healthy Families Act (SB 616 / Cal. Lab. Code § 246) All employers regardless of size Employees who work 30+ days within California within a 12-month period 1 hour per 30 hours worked; minimum statutory cap raised to 40 hours (5 days) per year Accrues Day 1; eligible to use on 90th day of employment Carryover of up to 80 hours (10 days) required unless frontloaded with 40 hours/5 days annually Diagnosis, care, or treatment of existing health condition; preventive care; domestic safety Paid sick leave is not payable upon separation (unless combined into general PTO bank)
New York (Statewide) September 30, 2020 New York State Paid Sick Leave Law (NY Labor Law § 196-b) All private employers across NY state with tiers based on net income and employee headcount All private sector employees in NY state 1 hour per 30 hours worked (Cap: 40 hrs for <100 employees; 56 hrs for 100+ employees) Accrues Day 1; usable immediately upon accrual (no statutory 90-day waiting period) All unused leave must be carried over; usage may be capped at 40 or 56 hours per year Mental/physical illness, injury, preventive diagnosis, family care, domestic violence/safety No payout required upon separation unless required by employer policy
Washington (Statewide) January 1, 2018 Washington Paid Sick Leave Law (RCW 49.46.210) All employers with non-exempt employees in Washington State All non-exempt employees covered by the Washington Minimum Wage Act 1 hour per 40 hours worked (no statutory annual accrual cap) Eligible to use on the 90th calendar day of employment Up to 40 hours of unused accrued leave carries over to the following year Illness, medical appointments, family care, public health closure of business/school, domestic violence Payout upon separation is not mandated by state law
